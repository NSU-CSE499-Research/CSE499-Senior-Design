ORBITAL LAB — NASA-inspired spaceflight learning prototype

1. Extract the whole ZIP.
2. Open the orbital-lab folder.
3. Double-click index.html.

No installation or internet connection is needed for the application.
For a quick presentation, scroll to the Flight Lab and select
"Watch a guided flight".
